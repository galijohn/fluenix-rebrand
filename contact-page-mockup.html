Strengths: the most personal of the two. Leadership is included naturally. The line "the same team you knew at FuelOmat" does the rebrand work on its own.
Trade-offs: adds a full section to the page. Seven cards need real photos for everyone — initials next to a professional headshot look weaker.
How the links work — and why
  • Phone numbers dial directly. On a customer's mobile, one tap on a number starts the call. Existing customers can still reach the person they know.
  • Email always goes to the team, not the person. Clicking a name, photo or email link opens a message to the team inbox the person belongs to — Gali and Jiří → sales@fluenix.eu, Krzysztof and Lucie → support@fluenix.eu, Ligia and Lenka → accounting@fluenix.eu. Each team inbox forwards to exactly the people shown.
  • Nothing gets lost. Every enquiry lands in one ordered place and reaches everyone on the team, so it is answered even when one person is travelling or on leave.
  • Enquiries can be measured. Because all email arrives through the three team inboxes, we can count enquiries per team, per week, and compare them before and after the rebrand. Personal inboxes would make that invisible.
