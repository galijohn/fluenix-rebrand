FuelOmat is becoming Fluenix. For more than 17 years, we have been helping companies take control of what happens around their fuel and fleet operations. What started with fuel management has grown into something much bigger. Today, our technology connects and automates critical infrastructure across mines, construction sites, airports, farms and fleet operations — from fuel dispensing and vehicle identification to access control, electric charging and more. We have grown from Europe into Africa, and now into Asia and other markets around the world. And as the company evolved, one thing became clear: Our name needed to evolve with us. FuelOmat described where we started. Fluenix describes where we are going. Same people. Same technology. The same commitment our customers have trusted for 17 years. But now, one name for a broader vision: Fluenix — Technology for Automation, Without Disruption. This is not just a new name.
