Step-by-stepKrok za krokem

How to set it upJak ji nastavit
  1. What Seznam is — the Czech search engine used by ~15–20% of Czech internet users (especially older demographics and public sector). Separate platform from Google Ads, usually cheaper per click.
  2. Open Sklik — Seznam's ad platform: sklik.cz. Create an account if none exists.
  3. Reuse the Google campaign — same keywords ("FuelOmat", "fuelomat.cz", "FuelOmat přihlášení" etc.), same landing page (fluenix.eu/formerly-fuelomat).
  4. Budget guide — start at ~15–20% of the Google Ads budget. Adjust after first week based on impression share.
  5. Monitor overlap weekly — some searchers show up on both; pause the more expensive channel per query.
Google + Seznam together cover essentially every Czech searcher.

How to build the audienceJak sestavit publikum
  1. What "remarketing" is — showing ads specifically to people who already interacted with us (visited the site, engaged with our social).
  2. Website audience (12 months) — analytics.google.com → Admin → Audiences → create "Last 12 months visitors, exclude customers".
  3. LinkedIn engagement audience — linkedin.com/campaignmanager → Matched Audiences → Company Page (last 365 days). Everyone who liked, commented, followed.
  4. Exclude existing customers — upload the customer email list from Mango CRM to each platform as an exclusion list. No wasted spend on people who already pay us.
Warm audiences convert 3–5× the cost of cold prospecting — this is where the launch pays off.
