Rebrand Manifesto

Why FuelOmat is now Fluenix

By Jan Kligl, CEO · 5 min read

Seventeen years ago, we started with one stubborn question: how do you actually stop fuel theft when the card-based systems everyone else used kept failing? That question turned into FuelOmat a.s. Today, that same team is stepping into a bigger role — and a bigger name. From today, we are Fluenix. Same people, same phone numbers, sharper mission.

Where we began

In 2008, fuel loss was not a marketing problem — it was a nightly reality on every industrial site in Central Europe. Cards were shared. PIN codes leaked. Reports lied. Fleet owners were bleeding fuel and had no way to prove it.

Our first installation went in at Pila Javořice in 2009 — a working industrial site that couldn't afford to be a science project. We had to make it work with pumps that were already there, gates that already worked, and machines that couldn't stop for a week. That constraint shaped everything we've built since.

By 2014 we were running 100 stations across the Czech Republic and Slovakia, connecting 2,500 vehicles. In 2017 we joined Novum Global. In 2022 we began refueling aircraft at Prague International Airport. In 2023 we shipped our first project in Africa. This year we crossed 850 stations worldwide, with 45,000 vehicles connected — and we're delivering 10–30% fuel savings to fleets that have been operating for decades.

Why our world got bigger than our name

The problem we solved in 2008 hasn't disappeared. But it stopped being the only problem our customers ask us to solve.

Today's fleets don't just consume fuel. They consume diesel and electricity. They protect depots, not just pumps. They manage access, energy, aircraft turnarounds, mining operations, agricultural cycles. Our customers don't want three separate vendors for fuel, access, and e-mobility — they want one platform that adapts to what they already have.

That's the work we're doing now: Fluenix Fuel, Fluenix Infrastructure, Fluenix Access, Fluenix E-Mobility, and Custom Fleet Systems — one modular platform, hardware-agnostic, built to sit on top of the equipment you've already invested in.

"FuelOmat" was honest about our origin. But it under-sold where we already operate.

Why the name Fluenix

Fluenix carries two ideas we care about.

Flow. Fuel flows. Energy flows. Fleets flow. When any of those stalls, an entire operation stalls with it. Everything we build exists to keep operations moving — without a rip-and-replace project, without downtime, without a two-year integration plan.

Reinvention. We ask legacy infrastructure to do more than it was designed to do — securely, transparently, and without being torn out. That reinvention, applied to a pump from the 1990s or a depot that was built before Wi-Fi existed, is the daily work.

Fluenix lifts our name above a single product category — because our customers have already asked us to.

What stays exactly the same

For our customers, employees, and partners — nothing that works today changes.

Every product runs as it did yesterday. Every integration keeps flowing. Every login opens the same account. Contracts stay in force — no re-signing, no migration, no downtime. Emails to @fuelomat.cz still arrive; @fluenix.eu is now our preferred address, but both work in parallel. Your account manager is the same person. Your support line is the same number. Your invoices simply say Fluenix at the top.

Same team. Same offices. Same commitment. New name.

What's ahead

Over the next year, the platform gets wider — not louder.

Deeper coverage where fleets already mix diesel and electric. Stronger access control for depots facing tighter compliance. Better analytics for the fleet managers who spend their week defending numbers to auditors. And continued expansion in the environments where our systems already earn their keep: aviation, mining, agriculture, heavy industry — places where downtime is not a rounding error.

More of what most of you tell us matters most: a partner that grows with the site, not out of it. The customers who trusted a small Czech company in 2008 are still with us today, at 45,000 vehicles. That is the relationship we are doubling down on.

Thank you

To the customers who trusted a small Czech company in 2008 and are still with us: this evolution is yours. To the team that has built this in warehouses, on airfields, in mines, at –20°C and +40°C: this name is yours. To the operators who told us honestly what didn't work: everything you're about to see reflects that feedback.

Welcome to Fluenix. Nothing that mattered has changed. Almost everything else got sharper.

Jan Kligl
CEO, Fluenix (formerly FuelOmat)
