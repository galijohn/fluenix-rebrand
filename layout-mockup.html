⠿WHY — the story

FuelOmat is becoming Fluenix.

For more than 17 years, we have been helping companies take control of what happens around their fuel and fleet operations.

What started with fuel management has grown into something much bigger. Today, our technology connects and automates critical infrastructure across mines, construction sites, airports, farms and fleet operations.

Our name needed to evolve with us.
